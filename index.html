About Carlos Perez

Bridging Global Enterprise Strategy with Southwest Florida Real Estate

Carlos is a seasoned professional who brings a rare, powerful perspective to real estate and private lending. Before focusing full-time on Florida real estate, he spent nearly three decades in international corporate leadership with IBM Corporation and Kyndryl, leading complex multimillion-dollar negotiations, digital transformations, and strategic partnerships for global institutions worldwide.

Simultaneously, Carlos has been an active, hands-on real estate investor for over 30 years. As founder of Alfa Management Group LLC, he built and managed a 100+ door rental portfolio across Florida and metro Atlanta, structured over $25M in transactions, and funded more than 200 private money loans.

Today, as a licensed Realtor® with Fine Properties, Carlos applies this rigorous corporate-level analytical thinking, creative deal structuring, and consultative care to help homeowners, retirees, and investors achieve exceptional outcomes in Sarasota, Bradenton, and Lakewood Ranch.
